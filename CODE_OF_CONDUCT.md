# Code of Conduct

SIF is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and abusive conduct are not tolerated.

Report incidents privately to the project maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially and may result in warnings, removal from discussions, or loss of contribution privileges.
